Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 6, 2026

FluffBooth ("we", "us") turns a photo of your pet into a stylized portrait. We designed it to know as little about you as possible. This page explains exactly what we process and why.

1. Your photos — processed, then gone

  • The photo you upload is sent from your browser to our server and forwarded to our image-generation provider (Google Gemini API) solely to create your portrait.
  • We do not store your photos or the generated results on our servers. Images travel through memory and are returned straight to your browser.
  • We do not use your photos to train any AI model.

2. Account data (only if you sign in)

  • Signing in with Google shares your name, email address, profile picture, and a Google account ID with us. We use these to run your daily free quota, purchased credits, referral rewards, and (if you opt in) a monthly new-styles email.
  • You can use FluffBooth without an account — you just get a smaller daily free quota.

3. Payments

Payments are processed by Stripe. Your card details never touch our servers; we only record that your credits were added.

4. Analytics

We keep anonymous counters (page opens, generations per style, downloads) to improve the product. Your IP address is used transiently to enforce the daily free limit and is stored only as a short-lived hashed counter — never linked to your identity or photos.

5. Cookies

One session cookie keeps you signed in (30 days), and a short-lived cookie remembers a referral code if you arrived through an invite link. No advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

6. Service providers

Google (sign-in and image generation), Stripe (payments), Upstash (account and quota storage), Vercel (hosting). Each receives only what is necessary to provide its function.

7. Your choices

  • Unsubscribe from emails anytime via the toggle in your account menu or the link in any email.
  • To delete your account data (quota records, credits, referral data), email us and we will erase it within 30 days.

8. Children

FluffBooth is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

9. Changes & contact

We will update this page if our practices change. Questions or deletion requests: support@fluffbooth.com.
